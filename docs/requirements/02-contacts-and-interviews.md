# 02. Contacts and interviews

Status: **Built**

- **Contacts are shared people, not per-application copies.** A contact (name, role, email, phone, LinkedIn link, notes; the LinkedIn link records where an application started when it came from a LinkedIn message) is stored once and attached to every application they appear on. This matters most for external headhunters, who show up on many applications: their details are entered once and cannot drift apart.
  - Adding a contact to an application (web, API or assistant) either **reuses a known contact** (`contactId` in the API) or takes new details.
  - **Web:** while typing a new contact's name, a banner suggests known contacts with a similar name (case-insensitive, one name containing the other, at least 3 characters) with a "Use this contact" button. Nothing is merged unless the user clicks it.
  - **Automatic reuse (API and assistant):** new details whose email (case-insensitive), phone number (digits only, +972 folded to 0) or LinkedIn profile match a known contact reuse that contact instead of creating a duplicate; the known contact's stored details win. **A name alone never merges** (two different people can share a name). The assistant's `add_contact` reports similar-named contacts in its result so it can switch to the existing one, and its tool descriptions tell it to call `list_contacts` first.
  - Editing a contact changes it on every application. The edit form says so when the contact is on more than one application.
  - Removing a contact from an application only detaches it. A contact left on no application (including when the last application is deleted) is deleted.
  - A contact on more than one application shows an **"On N applications"** button that opens the contact's page (`/contacts/:id`): their details and all of their applications with status. There is no page listing all contacts.
- Each application also has interviews (type, date and time, duration, location, notes). Interview notes are separate records attached to each interview (not a field on it), with a per-application Notes view (see [10 Interview notes](10-interview-notes.md)).
- Interview types: coffee chat, HR, phone, technical, system design, home assignment, home assignment review, onsite, managerial, final, other.
- An application can have several interviews of the same type, including back to back (for example two phone interviews). Interviews are independent records with no uniqueness rule on type; each is scheduled, edited and removed separately.
- **An interview round is one record, upcoming or past.** There is no separate "scheduled" entity: an interview is upcoming when its date and time are in the future and past otherwise. The Interviews section marks each one Scheduled or Past, and the timeline on the application page shows the same interviews in date order with future ones dashed and labelled Scheduled.
- **An interview is the round.** Every interview type has a stage except "other": coffee → coffee chat, HR → HR screening, phone, technical or system design → technical, home assignment or its review → home assignment, onsite, managerial, final. An application's status is derived from its interviews (see "Status" in [01](01-applications.md)): the next upcoming interview with a stage, otherwise the latest one held. Scheduling, moving or removing an interview therefore changes the status shown, and nothing else has to be kept in sync.
- **Choosing a round as the status adds an interview.** Picking a round in the status dropdown or the pipeline, or giving one to the API or the assistant tool (`interviewAt` optional, default now, past or future), creates the matching interview. It never reopens a closed application.
